# HB-HR-02: Leave and handover

Synthetic document. Flyvi Technologies people handbook. Owner: Hithesh.

1. Before planned leave of more than one week, every open task the person owns is handed over to their named backup, or explicitly paused with the requester's agreement.
2. Each item, customer or build the person owns gets an interim owner for the leave period.
3. Projects they lead name an interim lead.
4. Knowledge only the person holds (recent decisions, test results, supplier context) is written down and linked in the handover note.
5. A member of the leadership team confirms the handover before the leave starts.

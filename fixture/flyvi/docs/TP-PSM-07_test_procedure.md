# TP-PSM-07: PSU thermal and load qualification

Synthetic document. Owner: Satish (Quality).

## Scope

Required for any change to the regulator, inductor, or output capacitors on PSM-28, and for any new regulator candidate.

## Procedure summary

1. Full-load thermal soak at 70C ambient in a sealed enclosure.
2. Cold-start test at -20C with firmware power sequencing enabled.
3. Load-step response and ripple check.

## Effort

A full requalification takes 3 working days of lab time. Internal estimate: ₹8,000 per candidate part, covering chamber time and technician hours.
